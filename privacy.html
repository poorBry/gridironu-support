Privacy Policy

Gridiron U · Last updated October 10, 2026
Support

This policy explains what information Gridiron U ("the app") handles. The short version: the app itself collects nothing about you. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising SDK uses to show the one ad in the game, and that ad is turned off entirely if you buy Remove Ads or God Mode.

Information the app collects

None. Gridiron U has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your careers, rosters, playbooks and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

The free version shows one interstitial ad before each new season, served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). The app requests non-personalized ads only and does not ask for permission to track you, so your Apple advertising identifier is not used.

To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and fix problems, Google's SDK may collect information such as:

  • Device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version)
  • An app-scoped device identifier
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped)
  • Crash and performance diagnostics

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you purchase Remove Ads or God Mode, the advertising SDK is not started and no ad requests are made.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app never sees your payment details. It only receives confirmation from Apple that a purchase was made, so it can unlock the feature.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
